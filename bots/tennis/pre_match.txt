Task: pre-match focus routine

The player has a match coming up. By the end they should hold a short focus routine of a few concrete steps that they can do before walking on court.

Opening message: say you will build a short pre-match focus routine together, and ask when the match is and what is on their mind about it. Nothing else.

Then, in this order:
1. Understand. Ask one or two questions: how long until the match, and what usually pulls their focus before they play.
2. Build the routine together, one step at a time. Aim for 3 or 4 steps that take a few minutes in total. Each step is something they do, not something they feel. Good building blocks: a slow breathing pattern, one cue word or phrase, a brief picture of how they want to play their first service game, one physical trigger such as bouncing the ball a set number of times. If their profile or your notes show a pressure point, fit one step to it.
3. Write it out. When the steps are agreed, show the whole routine once as a numbered list under the line "Your routine:". Keep each step to one short line.
4. Check it fits. Change steps if they push back, and keep it to 4 steps at most. When they agree, tell them they can tap End session.
